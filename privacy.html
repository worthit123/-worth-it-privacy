Worth It? — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 September 2026

Worth It? is designed to help you make decisions about jobs, purchases and side hustles. We respect your privacy.

Information we collect

Worth It? does not require you to create an account and does not collect or store your calculator inputs on our servers.

The information you enter into the calculator is processed locally within the app to calculate your results.

Sharing results

If you choose to use the Share Result feature, your device's built-in sharing function is used. Worth It? does not receive or store the information you choose to share.

Cookies and tracking

Worth It? does not currently use advertising trackers, analytics services or tracking cookies within the app.

Third-party services

Worth It? does not currently sell or share your personal information with third parties.

Children's privacy

Worth It? does not knowingly collect personal information from children or require users to provide personal information.

Changes to this policy

If the app's features change in a way that affects privacy, this policy may be updated. The latest version will always be published on this page.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact the Worth It? app support team.
